Day-wise Itinerary

Welcome to Hanoi! Upon arrival, you'll be transferred to your hotel in the city center.

In the evening, enjoy a guided walking tour through the bustling Old Quarter and a welcome dinner.

  • Airport transfer
  • Old Quarter walking tour
  • Welcome Dinner

Explore the Ho Chi Minh Complex, the Temple of Literature, and Hoan Kiem Lake.

End the day watching a traditional Vietnamese Water Puppet Show.

  • Full day city tour
  • Water Puppet Show tickets
  • Breakfast included

Travel to the world-famous Ha Long Bay. Board your cruise and sail among the limestone islands.

Enjoy fresh seafood, kayak through hidden caves, and watch a glorious sunset over the bay.

  • Luxury overnight cruise
  • Kayaking & swimming
  • All meals on board

Start your day with Tai Chi on the deck followed by further exploration of the bay.

Disembark in the afternoon and transfer back to Hanoi for a free evening of shopping.

  • Tai Chi session
  • Cave exploration
  • Transfer to Hanoi

Enjoy your last breakfast in Vietnam and pick up some last-minute souvenirs.

Transfer to Noi Bai International Airport for your departure flight.

  • Breakfast included
  • Private airport transfer
