Day-wise Itinerary

Welcome to Switzerland! Arrive at Zurich Airport and take a private transfer to your hotel.

Enjoy an evening walk around Lake Zurich and a welcome fondue dinner.

  • Private Airport Transfer
  • Lake Zurich walk
  • Welcome Swiss Fondue

Travel to Lucerne and ascend Mount Titlis via the rotating cable car.

Experience the Cliff Walk and Glacier Cave before returning.

  • Lucerne City Tour
  • Mt Titlis Cable Car
  • Cliff Walk tickets

Take the scenic GoldenPass train route to the resort town of Interlaken.

Enjoy free time to explore the lakes or try paragliding.

  • GoldenPass Train
  • Interlaken stay
  • Breakfast included

Board the high-speed TGV train crossing the border into France and arriving in Paris.

Check into your Parisian hotel and relax.

  • TGV Train Tickets
  • Cross-country travel

Full-day guided tour covering the Louvre, Champs-Élysées, and Arc de Triomphe.

Skip-the-line access to the Eiffel Tower Summit for sunset.

  • Eiffel Tower Summit
  • Louvre Photo Stop
  • Seine River Cruise

Explore the bohemian district of Montmartre and the Sacré-Cœur Basilica.

Afternoon at leisure for luxury shopping at Galeries Lafayette.

  • Montmartre Tour
  • Free time shopping

Enjoy fresh croissants for your final breakfast.

Private transfer to Charles de Gaulle Airport.

  • Breakfast included
  • Private Airport transfer
