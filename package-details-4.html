Day-wise Itinerary

Welcome to Vietnam! Move to your hotel in Hanoi to settle in.

Get a first taste of the country with a local dinner in the Old Quarter.

  • Airport transfer
  • Welcome Dinner

Visit the Temple of Literature, Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, and the Old Quarter.

Enjoy the evening sampling world-famous Vietnamese street food.

  • City tour
  • Street food experience

Overnight cruise on the spectacular Ha Long Bay among towering limestone karsts.

Explore caves and take a refreshing swim in the emerald waters.

  • Luxury cruise
  • Kayaking
  • Seafood meals

Return to Hanoi and catch a short flight to Da Nang in Central Vietnam.

Transfer directly to the beautiful ancient town of Hoi An.

  • Domestic flight included
  • Transfer to Hoi An

Explore the historic Japanese bridge, Chinese temples, and local markets.

Free time in the afternoon for custom tailoring or beach relaxation.

  • Walking tour
  • Cultural immersion

Full day trip to Ba Na Hills to see the iconic Golden Bridge.

Return to Da Nang in the evening for a walk along the Han River.

  • Golden Bridge
  • Theme park access

Fly south to the bustling metropolis of Ho Chi Minh City.

Visit the Notre Dame Cathedral, Central Post Office, and Ben Thanh Market.

  • Domestic flight included
  • City orientation tour

Drive to the Mekong Delta and take a boat ride through the complex river system.

Visit local fruit orchards, coconut candy workshops, and honey farms.

  • Boat trip
  • Local village visit

Explore the incredible underground network of the Cu Chi Tunnels used during the war.

Enjoy a farewell dinner cruise on the Saigon River.

  • Cu Chi Tunnels tour
  • Dinner cruise

Last minute shopping in Ho Chi Minh City.

Transfer to Tan Son Nhat International Airport for your journey home.

  • Breakfast included
  • Airport transfer
